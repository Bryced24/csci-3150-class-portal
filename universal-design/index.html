Build websites everyone can use

Open Door teaches universal design to students and new web designers. Universal design means building one site that works for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

The web right now

1.3 billion people have a significant disability. That is about 1 in 6 people worldwide. WHO fact sheet, 2023

95.9% of the top one million home pages had accessibility errors that automated tests could find. WebAIM Million report, 2026

About this site

I made Open Door for ATLS-CSCI 3150: Universal Design at CU Boulder. The site follows the advice on the How page. I built the pages mobile first with semantic HTML and test each one with a keyboard and VoiceOver.
